Frequently Asked Questions About Air Duct Cleaning

Everything you need to know before ordering professional cleaning services for your HVAC system

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

It is recommended to clean air ducts every two to three years in residential buildings, and more frequently in commercial or industrial buildings. Regular maintenance ensures optimal air quality and prevents contaminant buildup.

Is duct cleaning suitable after renovation?

Absolutely. After renovation, dust particles, drywall, and construction material residue often remain in the system. Thorough cleaning removes all residue and restores the system to full and clean operation.

Do the ducts need to be dismantled for cleaning?

No. We use advanced equipment for in-system cleaning without needing to dismantle the ducts. This method preserves system integrity and significantly shortens work time.

Can work be performed in an occupied building?

Yes. The Maor Holdings team performs the work with minimal disruption to daily life. We maintain transparency, cleanliness throughout the work, and full coordination with the client throughout the entire process.

Does duct cleaning solve odor and mold problems?

Professional duct cleaning removes odor sources and organic contaminants. In cases of mold, we incorporate specialized disinfectants approved for use in HVAC systems that prevent regrowth.

When should I call a professional for an inspection?

If you notice a decline in air quality, unusual noises from the system, rapid dust accumulation, or an increase in energy consumption – it is recommended to schedule a professional inspection. Early diagnosis saves costly breakdowns down the line.
